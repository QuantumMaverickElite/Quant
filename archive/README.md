# Historical archive

This tracked tree preserves historical repository material that must remain
human-readable and recoverable but is not current runtime, package, or research
authority.

Current implementation and active research remain under
[`stock-backtester/`](../stock-backtester/README.md). Archive provenance and
verification records live under
[`stock-backtester/docs/reorg/`](../stock-backtester/docs/reorg/README.md).

Do not promote archived files into current ownership based only on their
presence here.
